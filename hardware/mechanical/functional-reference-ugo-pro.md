# Functional reference: ugo Pro

The cost-optimized product baseline uses the public ugo Pro product page as a
functional benchmark, not as permission to copy its appearance, protected
industrial design, drawings, software, branding, or proprietary mechanisms.

Official public claims reviewed on 2026-08-24:

- two seven-axis arms and an adjustable-height body;
- autonomous and remote travel;
- approximately 440 x 580 x 1800 mm and approximately 54 kg;
- approximately four hours continuous travel at 1.9 km/h and approximately
  eight hours continuous standby;
- approximately 0.4-3.6 km/h, five-degree grade and 10 mm threshold;
- 2D LiDAR, two depth sensors, ultrasonic sensing and automatic-charge marker;
- automatic charging is available.

Source: <https://ugo.plus/products/ugo-pro/>

Our design intentionally changes dimensions, structure, covers, interfaces and
safety provisions. The benchmark is used to challenge mass, footprint, energy,
sensor coverage and lifecycle cost. All performance must be independently
calculated and physically validated.
